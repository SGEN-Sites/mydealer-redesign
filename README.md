# My Dealer redesign

Website previews for My Dealer (Bayport, MN).

## Round 3

**Live:** https://sgen-sites.github.io/mydealer-redesign/round-3/

The earlier My Dealer preview, reskinned to the latest My Dealer brand card:
- **Color:** Near Black ground, Sand as the white, Gold as the accent only.
- **Type:** Montserrat for display, Figtree for body; the tagline in Montserrat Black capitals; display sizes fitted so headlines keep their line breaks.
- **Brand assets:** logo colorways, mark patterns and the menu-tile icons.
- **Unchanged:** the structure and copy.

The round's own changes:
- **Hero background:** made from the store's interior. It is an AI edit (fal Nano Banana Pro) of a store photo, color-graded to the site.
- **Pre-roll category image:** replaced.
- **Hero blinds overlay:** removed.
- **Index numerals on cards:** spaced clear of the headings.

The files under `round-3/` are generated by the reskin tools from the original preview, not edited by hand.

Preview only: every page is `noindex, nofollow`, and canonical URLs point to mydealermn.com.
