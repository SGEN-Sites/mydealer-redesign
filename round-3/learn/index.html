How we write these.

The way we'd say it at the counter. What the label says, what the law says, and where to check. Where a number matters, we link the source. Where the research is still early, we say so, and we don't make promises about what any product does.

Still have a question? Call the store at 651-323-1266, or ask a budtender in Bayport. We can talk products, formats, aroma and what's on the label. If something on a label doesn't add up, ask.

The fine print: these guides are general education, not medical or legal advice. We're not doctors, and we don't recommend cannabis for any condition.
